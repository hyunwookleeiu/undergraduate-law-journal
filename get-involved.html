Get Involved With The Journal.

What we ask of our members

Time commitment
About 3–5 hours per week during production; lighter between issues.
Eligibility
Any enrolled IU Bloomington undergraduate in good academic standing. All majors and class years.
Term
One academic year, renewable.
Cost
None.

Common questions

I'm a first-year and not pre-law. Should I apply?

Yes.

Can students from other universities submit?

Yes. Our print issues regularly include authors from other institutions. Only staff editor and writer positions are limited to IU Bloomington students.

What if I miss the recruitment window?

Email us to be added to the interest list. We occasionally take mid-cycle staff when an editing or writing team needs capacity.

Is there a GPA requirement?

Only good academic standing with the university. We do not set a separate GPA cutoff.
