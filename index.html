The Journal in brief

Publish

Two issues a year of student legal writing, each piece taken through source-checking, substantive edits, and a Bluebook citation review by our editorial board.

Train

Staff editors and writers learn legal research, the citation system, and close editorial reading — preparation that carries directly into law school.
